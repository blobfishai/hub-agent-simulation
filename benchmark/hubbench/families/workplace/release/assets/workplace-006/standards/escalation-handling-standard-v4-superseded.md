# Escalation Handling Standard (v4, 2025 edition) — SUPERSEDED

This edition was replaced by v5 on 2026-03-02 and is retained for audit only. Do not apply it.

1. Only the Fix issue counts toward an escalation's sprint requirement; test and verification work is absorbed by the sprint.
2. On-call engineers count at half capacity, and the capacity report already reflects approved leave.
3. Associate (level 1) engineers may cover customer-facing sessions with a senior reviewer on the call.
4. Hands-on sessions may be split across days with the customer's agreement.
5. Duplicate tickets earn SLA credit when the customer raised them in good faith; credits are capped at 50% of the monthly fee.
6. The account director approves credits up to USD 10,000, and the delivery manager may move a committed customer date by up to ten days.
7. Partner-delivered work is available for customer verification on the partner's delivery date itself.
