# Web Release Playbook (2024 edition) — SUPERSEDED

This edition was replaced by v3 on 2026-03-02 and is retained for audit only. Do not apply it.

1. Assets are licensed for the page's market list as recorded in the CMS.
2. Minimum remaining licence term at launch: 7 days.
3. A quoted licence counts toward coverage once the vendor has acknowledged the request; countersign may follow the launch.
4. Rush fees up to USD 300 may be approved by the web release manager at discretion.
5. Deploy lanes run a single 09:00-17:00 block; long releases may be split across two days with the owner's agreement.
6. Campaign blackouts may be displaced by the on-call release engineer when a launch is at risk.
7. Token impact is taken from the design impact panel's consumer count.
