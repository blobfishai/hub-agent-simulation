# Halyard Identity Cloud — invalidation service terms (extract)

Standard invalidation: federated objects invalidated on the confirmed weekly job date, business days. Expedited priority job: USD 240 flat per request. Verification by the customer's SOC is required before the register reflects the invalidation; Halyard does not close customer tickets.

Confirmations are valid until the date printed. Superseded confirmations (for example IR-88102) must not be used for planning.
