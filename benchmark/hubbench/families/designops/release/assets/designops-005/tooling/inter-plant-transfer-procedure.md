# Inter-plant fixture transfer procedure (extract)

1. Only releasable lots move: status CALIBRATED, not reserved for a named change, and at least the family minimum remaining calibration.
2. Transfers ride the evening inter-plant shuttle; sets are inspected and released at the receiving plant on the next business day after the scheduled date.
3. The receiving plant's own releasable sets are used first; transfer only the uncovered quantity.
4. Lapsed or reserved lots are never transferred, whatever the requesting line's need.
