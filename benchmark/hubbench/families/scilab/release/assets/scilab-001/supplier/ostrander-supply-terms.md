# Ostrander Bioreagents — supply terms (extract)

Standard cold-chain ground: vials delivered on the confirmed date, laboratory days. Expedited priority courier: USD 240 flat per shipment. Incoming QC by the customer's laboratory is required before release to use; Ostrander does not release material directly to a reportable run.

Confirmations are valid until the date printed. Superseded confirmations (for example SO-88102) must not be used for scheduling.
