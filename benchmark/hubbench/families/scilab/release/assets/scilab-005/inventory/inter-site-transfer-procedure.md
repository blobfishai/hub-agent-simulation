# Inter-site lot transfer procedure (extract)

1. Only usable lots move: status AVAILABLE, not reserved for a named run request, and at least the reagent's minimum remaining dating.
2. Transfers ride the 21:00 cold-chain courier run; vials pass incoming QC and are released at the receiving site on the next laboratory day after the scheduled date.
3. The receiving site's own usable vials are used first; transfer only the uncovered quantity.
4. Expired or reserved lots are never transferred, whatever the requesting study's need.
