# Corvane Certification Labs — service terms (extract)

Standard certification: certified runs ready on the confirmed date, business days. Expedited priority slot: USD 240 flat per request. Import and re-verification into the customer's release evidence is the customer's responsibility; Corvane does not release results directly to a deploy.

Confirmations are valid until the date printed. Superseded confirmations (for example CQ-88102) must not be used for scheduling.
