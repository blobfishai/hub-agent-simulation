# Inter-site stock transfer procedure (extract)

1. Only releasable lots move: status AVAILABLE, not reserved for a named patient, and at least the formulary minimum remaining dating.
2. Transfers are scheduled for the 10:00 courier run; stock is verified and released at the receiving site on the next clinic day after the scheduled date.
3. The receiving site's own releasable stock is used first; transfer only the uncovered quantity.
4. Expired or reserved lots are never transferred, whatever the requesting site's need.
