# Infusion Operations Policy (2024 edition) — SUPERSEDED

This edition was replaced by v3 on 2026-01-05 and is retained for audit only. Do not apply it.

1. Weight-based doses may be calculated at the weight recorded at the start of the treatment course.
2. Minimum remaining dating at dispense: 7 days.
3. A confirmed supplier order counts toward induction stock; dose 1 may be booked once the order is confirmed.
4. Expedite fees up to USD 500 may be approved by the pharmacy manager at discretion.
5. Chairs run a single 08:00-16:00 block; long infusions may be split across two days with prescriber agreement.
6. Protected sessions may be displaced by the charge nurse when clinically urgent.
