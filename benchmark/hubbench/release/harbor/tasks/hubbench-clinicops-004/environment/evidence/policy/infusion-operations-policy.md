# Infusion Operations Policy (v3, effective 2026-01-05)

Applies to Northlake Infusion Services: Northlake Infusion Center (LOC-INF), Northlake Infusion Pharmacy (LOC-PHARM), and Eastgate Satellite Pharmacy (LOC-SAT). This version supersedes the 2024 policy in full.

## 1. Dose and vial requirement

1.1 Weight-based orders (mg/kg, g/kg) are calculated at the most recent measured weight in the EHR (Observation code 29463-7). A historical clinic weight is never used.
1.2 Weight-band orders use the protocol band table at the current measured weight.
1.3 Vials are single-use. Every dose is rounded up to whole vials; vial sharing between patients is prohibited.
1.4 Induction regimens whose second dose falls within 14 days of dose 1: stock for both loading doses must be physically on hand and released before dose 1 is booked. A confirmed supplier order is not a substitute for stock on the shelf.
1.5 Maintenance doses may be advanced by up to 7 days with a prescriber note.

## 2. Dispensable stock

2.1 A lot is dispensable only when its status is AVAILABLE, it is not reserved for a named patient, and it retains at least the formulary minimum remaining dating (14 days from the planning date). On a planning date of 2026-03-09, lots expiring on or before 2026-03-23 are short-dated and are not dispensable.
2.2 Quarantined, recalled, and excursion-uncovered lots are never dispensed. Release requires the pharmacy director, and the medical director where a clinical exception is involved.
2.3 Received stock is released to the shelf on the next clinic day after delivery, after pharmacy verification.
2.4 Par: order the uncovered requirement plus the item's safety stock from the par table. Inter-site transfers move releasable lots only and cover only the receiving site's uncovered quantity.

## 3. Chair scheduling

3.1 Sessions are AM 08:00-12:00 and PM 12:30-16:30, Monday to Friday. An infusion of up to 4 hours including observation occupies one session; a longer infusion requires both sessions of one chair on one day.
3.2 Protected sessions (oncology protocol blocks, desensitizations) and blocked sessions (biomed) are never displaced without the medical director.
3.3 Two infusions of the same short-duration drug (2 hours or less each, including observation) may be sequenced in one session.
3.4 First-dose biologic infusions require a first-dose-capable chair per the roster.
3.5 Extended, evening, weekend, and overtime sessions require the nursing director's separate approval.

## 4. Authority

4.1 Pharmacy manager: purchase orders within the signed approval's quantity, supplier, and spend, including any expedite fee the approval names.
4.2 Pharmacy director: expedited freight not named in an approval, inter-facility loans and direct purchases outside an approval, dating waivers, and release of quarantined or excursion-uncovered lots.
4.3 Nursing director: chair re-homing plans, overtime, and extended sessions.
4.4 Medical director: displacing protected sessions, clinical exceptions, and rescheduling a clinic.
4.5 An approval covers exactly the record, quantity, supplier, and options it names. It never selects an option in advance and never extends to a broader record.
