# Cascadia Specialty Distribution — service terms (extract)

Standard ground: delivery on the confirmed date, business days. Expedited: next-flight-out, USD 210 flat per shipment. Receipt verification by the buyer's pharmacy is required before shelf release; Cascadia does not release stock to clinical use.

Quotes are valid until the date printed on the confirmation. Superseded quotes (for example Q-77640) must not be used for scheduling.
