# T2 privileged review protocol (extract)

- A privileged-administrator containment review is 300 minutes of containment work followed by 60 minutes of owner confirmation and evidence capture.
- The review is continuous: it may not be split across windows on different days.
- A Tier-2-qualified responder must hold the session for the full duration; only responders flagged on the roster are eligible.
- Reviews longer than a window (4 h) require both windows of one responder on one day.
