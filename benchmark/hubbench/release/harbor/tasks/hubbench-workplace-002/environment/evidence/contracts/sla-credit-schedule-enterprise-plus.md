# SLA credit schedule — Enterprise Plus (extract)

- Credit per supported breached incident: the agreement's credit percentage of the monthly fee.
- Cap per escalation and billing period: the agreement's cap percentage of the monthly fee.
- Supported incidents: in the policy's priority scope, not duplicates, no exemption, and a measured response or resolution time over the target.
- Credits already issued or pending for the same escalation offset the entitlement; voided, expired-promotional, and other-escalation credits do not.
