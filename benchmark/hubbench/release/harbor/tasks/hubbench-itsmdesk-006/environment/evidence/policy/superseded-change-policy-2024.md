# Change and Error-Budget Policy (2024 edition) — SUPERSEDED

This edition was replaced by v3 on 2026-02-01 and is retained for audit only. Do not apply it.

1. Planned downtime may be sized at the vendor's published estimate.
2. Error budget consumed is the raw SLI burn shown in telemetry; there is no reserve floor.
3. Tier-1 services may take weekday evening changes with the change manager's approval.
4. A vendor package may be deployed on its release date without a canary soak.
5. Any rostered secondary responder satisfies the coverage rule, certified or not.
6. Freeze windows may be overridden by the on-call primary when a vendor SLA is at risk.
