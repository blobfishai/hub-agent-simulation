# First-dose biologic infusion protocol (extract)

- First exposure to rituximab: titrated rate over 360 minutes followed by 60 minutes of observation.
- The infusion is continuous: it may not be split across sessions or days.
- A first-dose-certified nurse must be assigned for the full duration; only chairs with certified cover on the roster are eligible.
- Infusions longer than a session (4 h) require both sessions of one chair on one day.
