# Halyard Identity Cloud — device certificate invalidation terms (extract)

Standard invalidation: provider-issued device certificates invalidated on the confirmed weekly job date, business days. Expedited priority job: USD 210 flat per request. Verification by the customer's SOC is required before the register reflects the invalidation.

Confirmations are valid until the date printed. Superseded confirmations (for example IR-88390) must not be used for planning.
