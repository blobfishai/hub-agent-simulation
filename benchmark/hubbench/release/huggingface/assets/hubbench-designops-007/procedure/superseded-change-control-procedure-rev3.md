# Engineering Change Control Procedure ECP-12 (rev 3, 2024) — SUPERSEDED

This revision was replaced by rev 5 on 2026-03-02 and is retained for audit only. Do not apply it.

1. Affected assemblies may be counted from the last annual BOM export.
2. Minimum remaining calibration at cut-in: 7 days.
3. An open supplier order counts as sets on hand; the cut-in may be booked once the order is acknowledged.
4. Expedite fees up to USD 500 may be approved by the configuration manager at discretion.
5. A Class I change on a certified assembly may cut in under a 30-day interim deviation signed by the design engineer.
6. The line runs a single 07:00-15:00 block; long first-article runs may be split across two days with the owner's agreement.
7. Protected windows may be displaced by the shift lead when a cut-in is at risk.
